01 — About

What ARRO does

ARRO exists to make original robotics and AI research, and the education that leads into it, accessible outside the traditional institutional gate.

ARRO runs three connected tracks: an online education arm that teaches robotics, AI/ML, and programming from the ground up; a research and hardware track that builds original robotics and DIY projects; and a hackathon program that gives students a real venue to ship work.

Everything ARRO publishes — course material, research, hardware builds — is produced in-house and released under the ARRO name.

02 — Policy

The Arrotic designation

How original work produced under ARRO is named and attributed.

"Any theory, research, innovation, or invention produced by a member of ARRO carries the Arrotic name — alongside the name its creator gives it."

This is a naming convention, not a claim of erasure: the individual creator's own title for their work is always preserved and credited.

Theory

A framework, model, or explanatory idea developed by an ARRO member is designated an Arrotic Theory.

Research

A study, paper, or body of investigative work is designated Arrotic Research.

Technology

A tool, system, device, or piece of software is designated Arrotic Technology.

ARRO Innovation Foundation

Ideas · Innovation · Impact

AIF is the innovation-focused foundation of ARRO — the framework through which young innovators present ideas, receive guidance, gain recognition, and compete through two dedicated competitions: AIF Inspire and AIF Ignite.

Mission

AIF provides the structure for student innovators to be guided, evaluated, and formally acknowledged for original work. This rule book governs participation, submission, evaluation, selection, recognition, and guidance for both AIF Inspire and AIF Ignite. Inspire the idea. Ignite the innovator.

The two tracks

Open category

AIF Inspire

A competition designed to encourage innovation that can inspire, help people, solve a problem, create value, or introduce a meaningful new idea.

  • Any category of innovation, provided it meets AIF's originality and submission requirements
  • Open to individuals and teams, Playgroup through Class 12
  • Existing inventions may be improved and entered here
Focused category

AIF Ignite

A higher-difficulty competition intended to ignite the fire of innovation for humanity, the environment, or groundbreaking advancement.

  • Human safety innovation
  • Environmental safety innovation
  • Genuinely groundbreaking innovation — AIF determines whether an idea meets this threshold

Which track fits your idea?

Inspire example

Study-planner app

A mobile app that helps students organize their study schedule with reminders and progress tracking. Useful, original, and open to any category — a clear Inspire entry.

Ignite example

Flood-alert sensor device

A low-cost device that sends SMS warnings to riverside villages using water-level sensors. Directly addresses human and environmental safety — an Ignite-level entry.

A faster algorithm or a more advanced robot is impressive, but technical difficulty alone doesn't qualify it for Ignite — it has to clearly serve human safety, environmental safety, or be genuinely groundbreaking. Everything else belongs in Inspire.

Mentorship & what comes next

Guidance may be provided by ARRO members and, where available, relevant experts — covering idea development, technical reasoning, prototyping, and documentation. Mentoring doesn't guarantee selection or winning. Outstanding participants may also be considered for an internship, an ARRO project collaboration, or a contributor role — subject to suitability, availability, and ARRO's applicable rules.

Frequently asked

Can I submit more than one innovation?
Yes — a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 6 submissions per participant per year. Each individual innovation can only be entered into one track, either Inspire or Ignite.
Can I work in a team?
Yes, both individual and team participation are allowed. A team can have up to 9 members.
Do I need a working prototype?
No — a physical prototype is optional. A clear drawing or design of your proposed innovation is an acceptable substitute.
Can I use AI tools to help with my submission?
Yes, for coding support or writing your synopsis. But the underlying innovation, concept, and thinking must be your own — AI assistance doesn't transfer ownership of the idea.
How is AIF Ignite different from AIF Inspire?
Ignite is reserved for human safety, environmental safety, or genuinely groundbreaking innovation. Technical difficulty alone doesn't qualify an entry for Ignite — AIF determines whether an idea meets that threshold.
Will I definitely get the ₹1,000 prize if I win?
The cash prize is subject to ARRO's monthly revenue condition. If it doesn't apply that cycle, the winner still receives the applicable certificate and recognition.
What happens after I register on the website?
You'll receive your acknowledgment letter instantly. You then need to submit your full entry — synopsis, drawings/prototype, and code if applicable — through the AIF Submission Form, and comment your entry under ARRO Innovation's official AIF post to complete participation.
Does winning guarantee a role at ARRO?
No. Outstanding participants may be considered for an internship, project collaboration, or contributor role, but this is subject to suitability, availability, and ARRO's applicable rules.
